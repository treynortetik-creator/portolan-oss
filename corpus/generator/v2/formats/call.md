# Call transcript

A recorded phone or video call between the customer's staff and the vendor's staff, written out as a transcript.

## Output format

- One spoken turn per line, in this exact shape: `Full Name: what they say`
- Use the exact full names from PEOPLE for every turn. Only people listed in PEOPLE speak.
- Do not write timestamps, stage directions, headings, bullet points or blank lines. Timestamps are added afterwards.
- A turn is one line. A long turn stays on one line; never break a turn across lines.

## Realism

- Real calls wander: greetings and small talk first, then the substance, then scheduling and goodbyes.
- Turns vary a lot in length, from a two-word acknowledgement to a long explanation of several sentences.
- People interrupt, agree, back-channel and trail off. In roughly half of calls speakers use fillers and false starts; follow each speaker's voice notes.
- The vendor's staff ask follow-up questions and point to where screens or settings are, without saying what they show.
- Allowed color: greetings, weather, travel, screen-sharing trouble, who is joining, where a setting is, thanks and goodbyes. No next steps at the end.
