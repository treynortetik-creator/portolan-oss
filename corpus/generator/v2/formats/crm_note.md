# CRM activity note

A short note the vendor's account rep typed into the CRM after a touchpoint with the customer (a call, a visit, an email exchange).

## Output format

- Plain text lines. Put a blank line between lines so each line is its own paragraph.
- 2 to 6 lines in total.
- No headings, no bullet symbols, no field labels.

## Realism

- Rep shorthand: clipped phrases, abbreviations, first names or titles for people, little punctuation.
- The rep writes about what the customer said or did, in the third person.
- Allowed color: who was on the touchpoint and that it happened. No next steps, figures or plans outside the scripted sentences.
