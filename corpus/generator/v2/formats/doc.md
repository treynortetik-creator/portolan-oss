# Document

A working document the vendor shares with or writes about the customer: a renewal proposal, release notes, an implementation plan, a QBR summary, or a how-to guide.

## Output format

- Plain text. Paragraphs separated by blank lines.
- A short title line first is fine. Section label lines and bullet lines starting with `- ` are fine. One bullet per line.
- No markdown headings with `#`, no tables.

## Realism

- Business-document register: clear, structured, a little formal.
- Sections a reader would expect for this kind of document: purpose, scope, steps or changes, contacts.
- Allowed color: section headings, where to find things in the product, general process description. No figures, dates, versions, schedules or commitments outside the scripted sentences.
