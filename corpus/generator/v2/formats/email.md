# Email thread

A short email thread between the customer's staff and the vendor's staff. Each message replies to the one before it.

## Output format

- Each message starts with a line of the form `### Full Name` naming its sender, using the exact full name from PEOPLE.
- The lines after it are that message's body: a greeting line, one or more paragraphs separated by blank lines, and a sign-off.
- Do not write email headers (From, To, Date, Subject) and do not quote earlier messages with `>` lines. Headers and quoted text are added afterwards.
- When SUBJECT is requested, the very first line of the output is `Subject: <a short subject line>`, before the first `###` line.

## Realism

- Senders alternate between the two sides most of the time; a colleague is sometimes copied in and replies.
- Bodies range from one line ("thanks, will do") to several paragraphs.
- Each sender signs off in their own style (see their voice notes): initials, first name, full name and title, a full signature block, or nothing.
- Allowed color: greetings, thanks, where a setting is, out-of-office notes. No promises, next steps or dates outside the scripted sentences.
