# Support ticket

The comment history of one support ticket in the vendor's help desk, opened by or for the customer.

## Output format

- Each comment starts with a line of the form `### Full Name` naming its author, using the exact full name from PEOPLE.
- The lines after it are the comment body: one or more short paragraphs separated by blank lines.
- Do not write ticket fields (id, severity, status, dates). They are added afterwards.
- When SUBJECT is requested, the very first line of the output is `Subject: <a short ticket title>`, before the first `###` line.

## Realism

- The first comment says what the customer needs help with; support asks for details and points to where a setting is; the customer says where they looked.
- Comments are practical: which screen, which menu, a screenshot attached. No invented causes, findings, versions or serial numbers.
- Tone follows each author's voice notes; support staff are courteous and procedural.
- Allowed color: requests for a screenshot, questions about where a setting is, thanks. No findings, causes, fixes, versions, dates or promises outside the scripted sentences.
