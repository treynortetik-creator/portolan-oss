# Meeting notes

Internal notes from a vendor-side meeting about one customer account: QBR preparation, an account review, or a deal review.

## Output format

- Plain text. Paragraphs separated by blank lines.
- Short label lines (such as an attendee line or an agenda line) and bullet lines starting with `- ` are fine. One bullet per line.
- No markdown headings with `#`.

## Realism

- Notes are terse and written by one note-taker for colleagues who were in the room.
- Items are grouped roughly by agenda topic; action items name an owner but add no new promises beyond the script.
- Allowed color: attendees, agenda headings, room and dial-in logistics. No action items, owners, dates or figures outside the scripted sentences.
